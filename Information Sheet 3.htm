Title of Research Study:  Studying Human Design Decisions Using a Novel Directed Graph Representation

Investigator: Daniel Selva Valero

Why am I being asked to take part in this research study?

You are invited to participate in this study because we are trying to learn more about: design decision making and computational cognition.

You were selected as a possible participant in this study because you are a student in the Fall 2020 term of AERO 401 and Winter 2021 AERO 402 with knowledge of space systems. You must be 18 years of age or older to participate.  

Why is this research being done?

The purpose of this study is to observe design decisions. Participants will design earth observation missions using an offline Matlab application based on DAPHNE-EO. There will be two cases that we will look at. In one case the participants receive constant feedback. In the other case the participants will only receive feedback when they choose to. The participants will have up to 20 minutes for each of the two cases (40 minutes total). Participants may choose to submit their final designs earlier. We will aim to do two things with the collected data.

First, we will attempt to use the data to predicts design decisions. We will also attempt to characterizes design decision strategies.

Second, we will compare the behavior of the participants given constant feedback to their behavior when they must request feedback.

This study will examine design decision making from the perspective of computational cognition. In this case we have come up with a novel representation of the design decision tree. This approach allows us to study the path of a designer through a design space. We also hope to observe the effects of cognitive bias on design.

This study could enable the use of low cost methods for human machine teaming on design problems. We plan to explore the application of search methods on the decision space.

Additionally, we hope to gain some new insights into human design decision-making.

How long will the research last?

We expect that you will be in this research study for one hour.

What happens if I say “Yes, I want to be in this research”?

If you agree to participate in this study you will receive a link to download the application. You can access the application from your home computer or any computer on campus. You will perform a short tutorial to become familiar with the application as a tool. You will then participate in two separate design sessions of up to 20 minutes. If this is the first design session you will be prompted to start the second design session. If it is the second design then you will have completed your participation in the study.

During one of the sessions you will be given constant design feedback. During the other session, you will only receive feedback when you request it. The order of the two sessions will be randomized.

We will be tracking the actions you take in the application during the experiment. We will not collect any additional data. Individual participants will not be identifiable through the data.

The entire study should take no more than 60 minutes including the application tutorial.

You will be able to participate from any computer with a stable internet connection. The entire study will be performed remotely due to COVID precautions.

What happens if I do not want to be in this research?

Your participation in this study is voluntary. You can decide not to participate in this research and it will not be held against you.  You can leave the study at any time.
