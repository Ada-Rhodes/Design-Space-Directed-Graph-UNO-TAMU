# DSDG_Satellite_Study

The Design Space Directed Graph (DSDG) tool was developed as part of a collaboration between Dr. Ada-Rhodes Wish (nee Short) and University of Nebraska Omaha and Dr. Daniel Selva's lab at Texas A&M. It was presented at the 2022 AIAA SciTech Forum, and an experiment with it was published in their procedings https://arc.aiaa.org/doi/10.2514/6.2022-0925
